02 · Online application

Apply in minutes, from any phone.

Four short steps. The phone number is confirmed with a one-time code, so staff only spend time on real, reachable applicants.

1 · Loan: product, amount, term and purpose, with a live estimate.

2–3 · About you: identity, district, income and security offered.

4 · Confirm: consent and a 6-digit code. In the demo it shows on screen.

Done: a reference, the identity check and document upload, straight away.

Protected by design: rate limits on codes per number and per network, a hidden bot trap, server-side validation of every field, and consent recorded with the application.

03 · Identity verification

Verified in seconds, not days.

Straight after applying, the applicant photographs the front and back of their National ID and takes a guided selfie. On a computer, a QR code hands the camera step to their phone, and the result appears live on both screens.

  • Matched automatically: the photo on the ID against the selfie, plus the NIN and name typed in the form.
  • A clear result: verified instantly, or sent to an officer for review. Suspected fraud is blocked, and a bad photo asks for a retake.
  • Officers stay in control: review, override, text a fresh link, or verify a walk-in client in person.
  • No disbursement without a verified identity.
Provider: Smile ID Document Verification, used by banks and fintechs across Africa. The demo uses a built-in simulator that behaves like Smile ID's sandbox.

05 · Customer portal

Their loan, in their pocket.

Clients sign in with a code sent to their phone, with no passwords to forget. Routine questions ("what's my balance?", "when is my next payment?") no longer need a call or a branch visit.

  • Overview: balance, next payment, overdue warnings, progress and the saving from paying off early.
  • Schedule with paid, upcoming and overdue instalments, and every payment received.
  • Application tracker: received → under review → approved → funds sent.
  • Documents: upload what's missing and see why anything was rejected.
  • Account recovery for clients who change their number.
Security: codes are hashed, single-use, expire in 10 minutes and are rate-limited. The sign-in page never reveals whether a number belongs to a NANAM client.

06 · Mobile Money

Repay with MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, and it posts itself.

  • Pay the next instalment, the overdue amount, the full payoff or any amount. The client approves with their PIN on their own phone.
  • Money posts only after the network confirms it, then the client gets an SMS receipt with the new balance.
  • Safe by design: duplicate payments are rejected, overpayments open a refund case, and a sweep re-checks anything unresolved.
  • Statements reconcile every charge and payment down to a zero balance, ready to print or save as PDF.
Already wired up to MTN's and Airtel's official sandboxes. A Payments lab in the console tests each network end to end without touching any loan.

09 · Security & compliance

Built the way a regulated lender has to work.

Access

  • Role-based permissions and maker-checker
  • Hashed, httpOnly sessions; sign-in throttling
  • Staff password reset by email link
  • Disabled staff lose access immediately

Client data

  • Documents and ID photos in private storage, never public links
  • Files checked by their real contents (JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF)
  • Every staff view of a document is logged
  • Consent recorded with each application

Money

  • Payments post only on provider confirmation
  • Idempotent posting; duplicate references rejected
  • Overpayments become refund cases
  • Immutable audit log of every money movement

Regulation

  • 2.8% per month, the legal maximum (Legal Notice No. 21 of 2024)
  • Reducing-balance interest, fee shown before signing
  • Privacy, terms, key loan facts and complaints pages
  • Designed with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act in mind

10 · Try it yourself

Fifteen minutes, end to end.

Open {{HOST}} on a laptop, with your phone nearby. The lime Demo bar at the top means sample data: no real SMS, money or ID checks happen, and codes appear on screen.

A · Apply as a client
  1. Open /apply, choose a product and amount.
  2. Enter any name, a phone like 0772 555 123 and NIN CM12345678ABCD.
  3. On the last step, tap Use code in the green demo box, then Confirm & submit.
  4. Note your reference, then start the identity check.
B · Verify identity
  1. On a laptop, scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  2. Photograph the front and back of any card, then take the selfie.
  3. Watch the result appear on both screens.
  4. Try outcomes by applying with the surname Twinley (face mismatch), Xeroxley (officer review) or Blurton (retake).

  1. Open /portal/login and enter 0709 101 112 (Gloria, active loan).
  2. Tap Use code, then Sign in.

10 · Try it yourself · Staff console

Take your application all the way to payout.

Open /console/login. In the demo, the buttons under the sign-in card let you enter as any role with one tap.

  1. Loan officer: open your application under Applications and tap Assign to me & start review. Add the collateral (e.g. a land title valued at UGX 40M), tap Mark original received, write a short note and tap Recommend.
  2. Credit manager: open the same application. If the phone ID check from B wasn't done, use Record identity decision for an in-person check. Then choose Approve, give a reason and tap Record decision. Whoever recommended a loan can't approve it.
  3. Finance: enter the payout method and reference, then Confirm disbursement. The repayment schedule and the SMS to the client are created automatically.
  4. Open Loan book and record a repayment, or press ⌘K / Ctrl K and search a name.
  5. Administrator: see the Audit log of everything you just did, the Payments lab and SMS & reminders.
Try the guard-rails too: as the loan officer, open Team (not allowed); as the manager, recommend an application yourself and then try to approve it; try to disburse before the original collateral has been received.
